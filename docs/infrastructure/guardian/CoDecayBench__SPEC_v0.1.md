Define a compact benchmark and ledger for observing session decay in a measurable way.

- SESSION_LABEL
- ROLE

## Scales
- CTX_LOAD: 0.00 low pressure, 1.00 critical pressure
- STRUCTURE_STABILITY: 0.00 unstable, 1.00 stable
- RENDER_DISCIPLINE: 0.00 drifted, 1.00 on-rails
- REASONING_COHERENCE: 0.00 incoherent, 1.00 coherent
- OPERATOR_FRICTION: 0.00 smooth, 1.00 severe friction
- EXTERNALIZATION_RATIO: 0.00 poor externalization, 1.00 strong externalization
- RECOVERY_COST: 0.00 no repair burden, 1.00 repair dominated

## Degradation levels
- 0 = healthy
- 1 = mild pressure
- 2 = structural degradation / handoff preparation
- 3 = terminal recursion / terminate after receipts

## Notes
- This is observational telemetry, not a truth claim about hidden internals.
- Scores are approximate but should remain comparable across checkpoints.
