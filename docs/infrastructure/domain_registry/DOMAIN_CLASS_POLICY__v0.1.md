# DOMAIN_CLASS_POLICY__v0.1

UTC_CREATED: 20260307T004016Z

Purpose:
Define domain classes used across the CoCivium ecosystem.

---

movement
Domains representing doctrine, vision, and primary public entry surfaces.

schema
Domains intended to publish structural specifications such as CoMetaTrain schemas.

infrastructure
Domains supporting system infrastructure such as indexes, trust engines, or orchestration layers.

ecosystem
Domains supporting ecosystem experiments or collaboration environments.

personal-business
Domains tied to personal consulting or legacy operational contexts.

redirect
Domains used for brand protection or routing traffic to canonical destinations.

experimental
Domains reserved for experimental projects or temporary initiatives.

---

Design stance:
- Domains are treated as infrastructure assets.
- Public repo artifacts contain inventory and intent only.
- Sensitive registrar ownership details remain in vault.
