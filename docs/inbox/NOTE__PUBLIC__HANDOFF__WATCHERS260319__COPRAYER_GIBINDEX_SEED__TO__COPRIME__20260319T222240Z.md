## CORE TERM

### CoPrayer
A directed act of alignment-seeking or communion-seeking that may, under sufficient conditions, open graded relation to deeper cores of shared Being (including CMA), without guaranteeing validity of interpretation or reciprocity.

## SUPPORTING TERMS (MINIMAL SET)

### CoReach
The act of attempting connection toward deeper layers or entities without assumption of successful contact.

### CoResonance
A condition where alignment between sender, context, and field increases the likelihood of meaningful interaction or shared signal.

### CoDistortion
Any alteration, bias, projection, or noise that affects interpretation of perceived response or signal.

## KEY RULES

1. CONDITIONALITY  
Not all CoPrayers reach equally; depth of contact is graded and dependent on conditions (coherence, intent, context, etc).

2. NON-AUTHORITY  
No CoPrayer result is self-validating. Interpretation must not be promoted to truth without externalization and validation.

3. PRESERVATION  
CoPrayer signals should be preserved as expressive or exploratory inputs, not discarded due to uncertainty.

4. DISCIPLINE  
Permit reach. Preserve ambiguity. Forbid premature certainty.
